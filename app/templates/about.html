Legal & Regulatory Framework

Statutory Grounding & Public Service Rules

RetireClear strictly enforces the constitutional and statutory provisions governing the retirement of public and civil servants in the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

The Statutory Age Threshold (60 Years)

Pursuant to Public Service Rules (PSR) Rule 020810, the mandatory retirement age for civil servants is 60 years. RetireClear synchronizes original birth certificates, declared declarations of age, and personnel registers to identify age-based statutory clearance dates.

Automatic DOB reconciliation against first gazette.
The Service Milestone (35 Years)

Whichever comes earlier: civil servants must retire upon attaining 35 years of pensionable public service. Our algorithmic engine cross-matches initial letters of appointment, confirmation gazettes, and service progression ledgers to compute the exact clearance deadline.

Evaluated using the statutory "Whichever is Earlier" principle.
